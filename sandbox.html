Experiment #3 · Live

Wake Effect Sandbox

Every turbine leaves a "wake" of slower, choppier air behind it—park another machine in that shadow and it can lose 10–20%+ of its energy. Drag turbines, spin the wind direction, and watch farm output respond. Spacing is money.

→ Open simulator
