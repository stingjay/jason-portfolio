Prep Briefing

A researched, podcast-style briefing for a high-stakes conversation. Give it your side, their side, and the event, and it researches the other party, shows you the facts and an outline to approve, then writes a two-host episode and turns it into audio.

  • Every fact it uses has to pass a source check written in code.
  • Built around the event: a mock interview round, or an objection round and a clear ask.
  • Planned and scored as a real conversation, not a lecture read by two voices.
