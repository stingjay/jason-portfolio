HOW TO PLAY

Read the play. If a YOU marker appears, you're playing that position — tap where you should run, throw, or back up. Otherwise, you're the fielder with the ball — tap the base where you'd throw it.

Get promoted! Build a streak of correct plays to unlock the next age level. Rack up points: answer before the pitch clock runs out for a speed bonus, and keep a streak going for a combo multiplier (x1.5 at 3, x2 at 5, x3 at 10). The hint button never breaks your streak — it just skips the speed bonus.

Position cheat sheet: P = pitcher, C = catcher, 1B/2B/3B = first/second/third basemen, SS = shortstop, LF/CF/RF = left, center, right field. BACK circles mean "back up that base."
